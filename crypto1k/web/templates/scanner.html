How the score works

Each coin gets a 0–10 validity score from four weighted signals, then gets labelled and (optionally) alerted on.

Volume surge max 4.0
  • 1h volume ≥ 5.0× its 24h average → full 4.0 pts (strong)
  • 1h volume ≥ 2.5× average → 2.4 pts (notable)
  • otherwise a small scaled amount, up to 0.6 pts
  • a 5m acceleration (≥3.0×, or ≥6.0× for full marks) can push this to the max — catches a move before the 1h window catches up
  • direction (bullish/bearish) follows the price move in that window; volume itself is directionless
Momentum max 3.0
  • |1h price change| ≥ 8% → 1.8 pts; ≥ 3% → 1.05 pts
  • |5m price change| ≥ 3% → +1.05 pts; ≥ 1.5% → +0.6 pts
  • alignment bonus: 5m, 1h and 6h all moving the same direction → +1.2 pts
Buy pressure max 2.0
  • buy/sell ratio (1h) ≥ 2.5:1 → full 2.0 pts
  • ratio ≥ 1.5:1 → 1.0 pt
  • ratio ≤ 1/1.5 (sellers dominating) → 0 pts, flagged as bearish "sell pressure"
Activity / liquidity max 1.0
  • liquidity ≥ 3× min AND txns ≥ 3× min → full 1.0 pt
  • merely passes filters → 0.5 pt
Smart money bonus, max 1.5
  • a tracked smart wallet bought (last 60m) → +1.5 pts and can fire a smart alert on its own
  • whale net flow ≥ +$20K in 60m → +1.0 pt; ≥ +$5K → +0.5 pt
  • single whale buy ≥ $2.5K → +0.5 pt
  • whale = trades ≥ $500; wallets are auto-promoted to "smart" when ≥5 scored buys hit ≥55% win rate (+5% in 1h or +10% in 24h) — see the Smart Money page
Market structure info + caps
  • 24h volume ≥ 2× market cap → "actively traded" signal (≥5× = strong)
  • liquidity < 3% of market cap → thin exit door: score capped at 6.0 and flagged — you can't get out without moving the price

Wash-trade cap: if 24h volume ÷ liquidity ≥ 75×, the score is capped at 6.0 (GOOD) and flagged regardless of the raw total. The same cap applies when liquidity is under 3% of market cap (thin exit).

Filters (gates): a coin isn't scored at all unless liquidity ≥ $15,000, 24h volume ≥ $20,000, ≥15 txns in the last hour, pair age ≥ 1h, and its 1h price change looks sane (≤ 5,000% — guards against corrupted feeds).

Alerting is separate from scoring: an email/Telegram alert fires when score ≥ 7.5 and a real volume surge is present (1h ≥ 2.5× or 5m ≥ 4.0×) — or via a fast path that alerts on an extreme 5-minute spike (volume ≥ 6×, price move ≥ 2%, buy/sell flow ≥ 1.5:1) independently of the score, since the score leans on 1h windows that can lag a fresh move by 30–60 minutes.
