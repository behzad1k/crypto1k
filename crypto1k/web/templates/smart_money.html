How this works

Every scan also pulls the individual trades (with wallet addresses) for coins that score well. Whale-sized trades are recorded; each buy is scored 1 h and 24 h later from on-chain candles. A wallet whose buys keep preceding pumps auto-qualifies as smart money — from then on, its buys add a score bonus in the scanner and can fire a smart alert on their own.

Coverage: wallets are observed on the pools your scanner watches (watchlist + alerted coins) — not globally across every chain. Add wallets you trust manually to widen the net.
